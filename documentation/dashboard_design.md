# Dashboard Design

## Overview

The Power BI dashboard was designed to provide a clear and interactive view of the Superstore's sales performance.

The objective of the dashboard is to help users quickly understand key business metrics, identify patterns in sales performance, and support data-driven decision-making.

The dashboard focuses on two main analytical areas:

- Sales Performance Overview
- Sales Trends and Forecasting

---

## Dashboard Structure

The dashboard is divided into two main sections to improve readability and guide users through the analysis.

### 1. Sales Performance Overview

This section provides a high-level summary of overall sales performance.

Key elements included:

- **Total Sales KPI** – Displays the overall revenue generated from all transactions.
- **Total Orders KPI** – Shows the total number of orders in the dataset.
- **Sales by Category (Bar Chart)** – Highlights which product categories generate the highest revenue.
- **Sales by Region (Map or Bar Chart)** – Displays geographic sales distribution.
- **Top Products by Sales (Bar Chart)** – Identifies the highest revenue-generating products.

This section allows users to quickly understand where revenue is coming from and which business areas perform best.

---

### 2. Sales Trends and Forecasting

This section focuses on analyzing sales patterns over time and identifying potential future trends.

Key visualizations included:

- **Sales Trend Over Time (Line Chart)** – Displays daily or monthly sales performance.
- **Monthly Sales Analysis** – Helps identify seasonality and long-term sales patterns.
- **Sales Forecast (Power BI Forecast Feature)** – Estimates expected sales for the next **7 days** based on historical trends.

These visualizations help businesses anticipate demand and make informed planning decisions.

---

## Design Principles

Several dashboard design principles were applied to improve clarity and usability.

### Simplicity

The dashboard focuses only on essential metrics and avoids unnecessary visual clutter to improve readability.

### Clear Visual Hierarchy

Key performance indicators such as **Total Sales** and **Total Orders** are placed at the top of the dashboard to immediately highlight overall business performance.

### Consistent Color Usage

A consistent color scheme was used across charts to maintain a professional appearance and improve visual interpretation.

### Interactive Filtering

Power BI slicers allow users to filter the dashboard by relevant dimensions such as time period, region, or product category, enabling deeper exploration of the data.

---

## Dashboard Objective

The dashboard enables stakeholders to:

- Monitor overall sales performance
- Identify top-performing products and categories
- Compare sales performance across geographic regions
- Understand sales trends over time
- Anticipate short-term future sales using forecasting

---

## Summary

The dashboard provides a structured and interactive view of the Superstore's sales data.

By combining key performance indicators, trend analysis, and forecasting visualizations, the dashboard enables users to quickly identify business insights and make informed decisions based on historical sales performance.
