# Analysis Process

## Overview

This document outlines the analytical workflow used to examine the Superstore Sales dataset and generate business insights and short-term sales forecasts. The analysis focuses on understanding historical sales performance across products, customers, and regions.

---

## 1. Data Understanding

The dataset contains transactional sales records from a global retail superstore. Each record represents an individual order containing information about:

- Order details
- Product category and sub-category
- Customer segment
- Geographic location
- Sales value
- Order dates

These variables enable the analysis of sales performance across multiple business dimensions.

---

## 2. Data Preparation

Before analysis, the dataset was reviewed to ensure consistency and usability.

The following preparation steps were performed:

- Verified column data types for consistency (dates, numeric values, and text fields)
- Checked for missing values in critical fields
- Validated order dates and chronological consistency
- Ensured sales values were stored as numeric data
- Confirmed that product and category fields were properly categorized

This step ensured the dataset was suitable for accurate analysis and visualization.

---

## 3. Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)

Exploratory analysis was conducted to identify patterns and trends in sales performance.

The following areas were explored:

### Sales by Product Category
Sales were aggregated by category to determine which product groups contribute the most revenue.

### Sales by Sub-Category
Sub-category analysis provided deeper insight into specific products driving sales performance.

### Regional Sales Performance
Sales were analyzed by region to identify geographic markets contributing the highest revenue.

### Customer Segment Analysis
Customer segments were evaluated to determine which groups generate the most sales.

### Sales Trend Over Time
Sales data was aggregated by date to observe monthly and yearly trends.

---

## 4. Dashboard Development

A Power BI dashboard was developed to visually present key metrics and insights.

The dashboard is divided into two sections:

### Sales Performance Overview
This section focuses on high-level performance indicators, including:

- Total sales
- Sales by category
- Regional sales comparison
- Top-performing products

### Sales Trends and Forecasting
This section focuses on temporal analysis and predictive insights, including:

- Monthly sales trends
- Moving average patterns
- Short-term sales forecasting

These visualizations allow users to quickly interpret business performance.

---

## 5. Sales Forecasting

A short-term forecast was generated using historical sales data.

The objective of the forecast is to estimate expected sales for the **next 7 days** based on observed trends.

Time series patterns in the historical data were used to project future sales behavior, allowing businesses to better plan inventory and operational resources.

---

## 6. Insight Generation

After completing the analysis and dashboard design, key insights were derived from the data to answer the project’s business questions.

These insights focus on:

- Identifying high-performing product categories
- Understanding regional market performance
- Evaluating customer segment contributions
- Detecting sales trends and seasonal patterns
- Estimating short-term future sales

The final insights and recommendations are documented in the **analysis folder**.

---

## Summary

The analysis process combined data preparation, exploratory analysis, dashboard visualization, and forecasting techniques to generate actionable business insights from the Superstore sales dataset.
